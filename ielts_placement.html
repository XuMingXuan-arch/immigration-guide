📖 阅读 Reading 自动算分

共 12 题（Heading 匹配 / 判断 / 选择 / 填空）。建议 18 分钟内完成。完成后点下方「批改阅读」。

A. In the 15th century, Johannes Gutenberg's invention of movable-type printing transformed Europe. Before this, books were copied by hand — a slow process that made them expensive and rare. Gutenberg's press, developed around 1440 in Mainz, Germany, allowed texts to be produced quickly and cheaply. Within decades, printed books spread across the continent.

B. The impact was profound. Literacy rates gradually rose as books became accessible to ordinary people. Religious texts, previously controlled by the Church, could now be read and interpreted privately. This contributed to major movements such as the Reformation. Scientific ideas also circulated faster; scholars could build on each other's work without waiting years for manuscript copies.

C. However, the printing press did not instantly create a reading public. In the early years, most printed material was still in Latin, limiting its audience. It took more than a century for vernacular languages to dominate printed books. Moreover, the cost of a book, while lower than a handwritten one, remained beyond the reach of the poorest families for generations.

D. Today, historians regard the printing press as one of the key inventions that shaped the modern world, comparable in importance to the internet in our own time.

🗣️ 口语 Speaking 自评

雅思口语分三部分。下面给出真实考题，请口头作答后凭流利度/词汇/语法自评。

Part 1 介绍类（每项用 2–3 句回答）
1. What do you usually do in your free time?
2. Do you prefer studying in the morning or at night? Why?
3. Tell me about your hometown.

Part 2 个人陈述（准备 1 分钟，说 1–2 分钟）
Cue card: Describe a person who taught you something important. You should say: who the person is, what they taught you, and why it was important to you.

Part 3 讨论类（深入回答）
1. Why is it important for young people to learn a foreign language?
2. Do you think schools should make English compulsory? Why or why not?
